## Description
Lily Leap is a quick, one-thumb arcade hopper set on a moonlit pond.

Tap to hop your frog forward one row. Swipe left or right to jump to the next lily pad. Rows of lily pads flow across the river in alternating directions, so time every hop carefully. Miss a pad and a hungry crocodile is waiting below. Stay too long and your pad will sink. Ride a pad off the edge and you'll be swept away!

• Simple controls: tap to hop, swipe to move sideways
• Flowing rows of lily pads that speed up as you go
• Glowing fireflies for bonus points
• Calm rows every so often to catch your breath
• Sound effects with a one-tap mute
• Short runs, perfect for a quick break
• Plays offline, no account needed

## App Review notes
Purpose and audience: Lily Leap is a casual arcade game for anyone aged 4+. The player guides a frog across a pond by hopping onto rows of flowing lily pads. Tap to hop forward one row; swipe left or right to hop to the neighbouring pad. Missing a pad, staying on a sinking pad, or being carried off-screen ends the run. Fireflies give bonus points.

How to access the main features: No login, account, or setup is required. Open the app and tap to start hopping. When a run ends, the player can choose "Watch ad · get a rescue pad" to continue once, or "Hop again". The best score and the sound setting are stored on the device only.

External services: Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for a rewarded video ad (only when the player chooses to continue) and an interstitial ad at most once every 3 games, between games. Google's User Messaging Platform is used for ad consent where required. No other external services, accounts, payments, or AI services.

Regional differences: The app functions identically in all regions where it is available.

Regulated industry / third-party material: Not applicable. All artwork, sound, code and content are original.
